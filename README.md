# MEKAKEL (መካከል)

> **Finding blood when someone needs it.**

MEKAKEL is a web-based blood coordination platform designed to help authorized healthcare facilities identify potentially compatible and available blood sources more efficiently.

The platform connects:

* Healthcare facilities
* Blood inventory
* Verified volunteer donors

## Project Goal

MEKAKEL provides a digital coordination platform for managing blood requests, hospital inventory, and verified donors. It aims to improve communication and reduce delays while maintaining appropriate controls for blood compatibility, screening, verification, reservations, and medical information.

## Why MEKAKEL?

Access to safe blood at the right time can be the difference between life and death. However, locating available blood and coordinating with potential donors can be challenging.

MEKAKEL addresses this challenge by bringing healthcare facilities, blood inventory, and verified volunteer donors into one coordinated system, helping healthcare professionals find potential blood sources more efficiently.

## Key Features

* User registration and authentication
* Role-based access control
* Donor registration and profile management
* Hospital and clinic management
* Blood request management
* Blood inventory management
* Potential donor identification
* Donor medical report submission and verification
* Reservation management
* Email and in-app notifications
* Hospital-to-hospital and clinic-to-clinic coordination

## Important Note

MEKAKEL is a blood coordination platform, not a blood bank. It does not collect, store, or test physical blood. Medical eligibility and blood compatibility must be confirmed by authorized healthcare professionals.
